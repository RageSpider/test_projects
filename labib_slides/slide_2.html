Sec.03 / AttentionSpeaker 03 / Irfan Sadik

Attention and scrolling.

Social media gives us a continuous flow of new content. We can move from one video or post to another within seconds.

  • A-01Notifications can interrupt us while we are studying, working, or even talking to someone.
  • A-02Constant switching and frequent interruptions make it harder to stay focused on one task.
  • A-03This does not mean social media permanently damages our attention.
  • A-04For students this matters, because studying often requires long periods of concentration.
Focus levelFIG.04
Deep study90%
After interruptions45%
Switch costMEASURED
Every notification pulls attention away. Each switch costs time and focus to recover.

Emotions and self-image.

Social media can also affect our emotions and how we see ourselves.

  • E-01We mostly see people's happy moments. We do not always see their real struggles.
  • E-02When we compare our life with what we see online, we may sometimes feel we are not doing well enough.
