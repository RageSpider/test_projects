Why we keep checking.

Social media also gives us unpredictable rewards. Sometimes we get many likes or an interesting video, and sometimes we don't.

  • i.This uncertainty can encourage us to keep checking our phones.
  • ii.That is why we sometimes open social media without even planning to.

Attention and Scrolling
03.
Irfan Sadik
Speaker 3

Attention and scrolling.

Now, let's talk about attention. Social media gives us a continuous flow of new content.

  • i.We can move from one video or post to another within seconds.
  • ii.Notifications can interrupt us while we are studying, working, or even talking to someone.
  • iii.This does not mean social media permanently damages our attention. However, constant switching and frequent interruptions can make it harder to stay focused on one task.
  • iv.For students, this can be especially important, because studying often requires long periods of concentration.

Maruf Hasan

Emotions and self-image.

Let's talk about emotions and self-image. Social media can also affect our emotions and how we see ourselves.

  • i.We mostly see people's happy moments in social media. We do not always see their real struggles.
  • ii.So, when we compare our life with what we see online, we may sometimes feel that we are not doing well enough.

Conclusion

Use it intentionally, not automatically.

After looking at the effects of social media on our brain, one thing becomes clear. Social media is not necessarily our enemy, but our relationship with it matters. We can make small changes.

i.
Turn off unnecessary notifications.
ii.
Take short screen breaks.
iii.
Avoid social media when we need to concentrate.
iv.
Avoid social media when we need to sleep.
