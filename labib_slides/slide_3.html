06 Attention Irfan Sadik

Attention and scrolling.

Social media gives us a continuous flow of new content. We can move from one video or post to another within seconds.

  • 06Notifications can interrupt us while we are studying, working, or even talking to someone.
  • 07Constant switching and frequent interruptions make it harder to stay focused on one task.
  • 08This does not mean social media permanently damages our attention.
  • 09For students this matters, because studying often requires long periods of concentration.
