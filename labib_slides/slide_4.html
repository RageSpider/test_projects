Scene 06 · Act III · Attention · Irfan Sadik

Attention and scrolling.

Social media gives us a continuous flow of new content. We can move from one video or post to another within seconds.

  • v.Notifications can interrupt us while we are studying, working, or even talking to someone.
  • vi.Constant switching and frequent interruptions make it harder to stay focused on one task.
  • vii.This does not mean permanent damage. For students, it matters because studying needs long, unbroken concentration.
A study sessionINTERRUPTED
00:00Deep focus begins reading, problem solving
00:04PING · a notification arrives
00:05Focus returns weaker than before
00:11PING · another notification
00:12Focus returns even harder to hold
∞Each switch costs time to recover.

Emotions and self-image.

Social media can also affect our emotions and how we see ourselves.

  • viii.We mostly see people's happy moments. We do not always see their real struggles.
  • ix.When we compare our life with what we see online, we may sometimes feel we are not doing well enough.

Scene 09 · Act V · Conclusion · Labib Mirza

Use it intentionally, not automatically.

Social media is not necessarily our enemy, but our relationship with it matters. We can make small changes.

1Turn off unnecessary notifications.
2Take short screen breaks.
3Avoid it when you need to concentrate.
4Avoid it when you need to sleep.
